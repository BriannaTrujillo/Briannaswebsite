Green Bean Casserole

by Bri

A classic American casserole baked in a 9 by 11 cake pan, mixed with cream of mushroom soup, green beans, and hamerburger are combined to create a delicious dish topped with french fried onions and baked to perfection. (12 servings).

Ingredients

  • 4 cup green beans
  • 2 can (20.10 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 (8 ounce) packaged cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/2 cups french fried onions
  • 2 pounds cooked hamburger meat seasoned to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 450° F (230° C).
  2. mix chopped cooked green beans with the cream of mushroom soup.
  3. put the mixture in a 9 by 11 cake pan.
  4. in another bowl, combine the hamburger meat with the worcestershire sauce, soy sauce and black pepper and salt
  5. combine the two mixtures and pour into the cake pan
  6. top with french fried onions and cheddar cheese
  7. bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown

I like the crispy texture of the french fried onions with the cheesy sauce.
