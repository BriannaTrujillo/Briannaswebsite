Pumpkin Pie

by Brianna Trujillo

A classic American Pumkin Pie with a spiced pumpkin filling and a flaky crust. Topped with the finest maple syrup and a dollop of whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Canned Pumpkin
  • 1 Store Bought Pie Crust
  • 2 large Eggs
  • 1 Cup Heavy Cream
  • 3/4 Teaspoon Ground Cinnamon
  • 1/2 Cup Pure Maple Syrup
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Ground Ginger
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Salt
  • Pinch Of Cloves

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (230° C).
  2. mix together the pumpkin, eggs, and cream.
  3. mix in the spices and maple syrup.
  4. put the mixture in a store-bought pie crust.
  5. bake for 35-60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
